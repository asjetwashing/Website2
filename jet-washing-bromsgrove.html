Driveway Re-sanding in Bromsgrove — the finishing step

After a thorough jet wash, the high-pressure water naturally removes the jointing sand from between your block paving. This is completely normal — and it's our job to put it back properly.

We finish every block paving job by brushing kiln-dried sand back into the joints. This step is essential: without it, your blocks can shift, weeds take hold faster, and the surface loses its structural integrity over time.

  • 1 Jet wash removes old jointing sand along with dirt, moss and algae
  • 2 Kiln-dried sand is spread evenly across the clean, dry surface
  • 3 Sand is brushed into the joints and compacted for a firm, weed-resistant finish
  • 4 Any excess is swept away — leaving a clean, professionally finished driveway
