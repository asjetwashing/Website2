Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

How All Saints Jet Washing collects, uses and protects your information when you get in touch for a quote.

Last updated: 3 September 2026

1. Who we are

All Saints Jet Washing is a local exterior cleaning business serving Bromsgrove and surrounding areas, offering jet washing, gutter vac, fascia & soffit cleaning and window cleaning.

Contact us about privacy: Phone / Text / WhatsApp: 07940 188231. You can also reach us via our Contact page or Facebook page.

For UK data protection law, we are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

2. What information we collect

We only collect what we need to provide a quote and complete your job:

  • Contact details: name, phone number, postcode / address when you provide it.
  • Job details: description of what needs cleaning and any photos you send via WhatsApp, text, Facebook or Google lead forms.
  • Google Ads lead forms: if you tap our Google ad form, Google shares the name, phone number and enquiry details you enter with us.
  • Calls and messages: records of calls, texts, WhatsApp messages and Facebook messages you send us.
  • Website technical data: basic, anonymised logs such as pages visited, device type and approximate location, used to keep the site secure and working.

We do not collect payment card details through this website and we do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide free, no-obligation quotes.
  • To arrange, confirm and complete cleaning services.
  • To send service-related messages, for example confirming arrival times.
  • To improve our services and website, and to keep records for business administration.

Our lawful bases under UK GDPR are: performance of a contract or steps prior to entering a contract (providing quotes), legitimate interests (running our business and responding to enquiries), and consent where you have given it (for example, sending photos).

4. Hosting, Google Ads, WhatsApp and Facebook

This website is hosted and delivered through Cloudflare, which processes IP addresses and basic technical logs to keep the site secure, fast and reliable. See: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

We use Google Ads measurement (gtag.js) to understand when someone clicks our phone or WhatsApp buttons after seeing an ad. This sets cookies / uses local storage from Google to count those clicks as conversions.

If you contact us or submit a lead form through Google, WhatsApp or Facebook, those companies also process your data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google: Google Privacy Policy
  • WhatsApp: WhatsApp Privacy Policy
  • Facebook: Facebook Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare: Cloudflare Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for marketing by third parties.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry and job records only as long as needed for quotes, guarantees, accounts and legal obligations — typically up to 6 years for business records, then securely deleted.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to, and port your personal data, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To exercise any right, call or text 07940 188231. We will respond within one month.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels directly. Cloudflare may set essential security cookies to protect the site, and embedded services such as fonts or social buttons may set their own cookies if you interact with them. You can block cookies in your browser settings — the site will still work.

8. Security

We store enquiries on password-protected phones and accounts, limit access to those who need it, and never ask for passwords or bank details by text or WhatsApp.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be posted at allsaintsjetwashing.co.uk/privacy-policy.html.
